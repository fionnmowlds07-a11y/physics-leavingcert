An tEarrach Thiar

Máirtín Ó Direáin — Filíocht (Poetry)

Dán cumhachtach faoi ghrá áite, cuimhní óige, agus áilleacht oileán Árann. A powerful poem about love of home, childhood memories, and the breathtaking beauty of the Aran Islands in spring.

📜 An Dán / The Poem

An tEarrach Thiar · Máirtín Ó Direáin

Véarsa 1 · Verse 1
Fear ag glanadh cré
De ghimseán spáide
Sa gciúnas séimh,
Binn an fhuaim,
San Earrach Thiar.
A man cleaning clay / From the heel of a spade / In the gentle quiet, / Sweet is the sound, / In the Western Spring.
Véarsa 2 · Verse 2
Fear ag caitheamh
Cliabh dhá dhroim,
Is an fheamainn dhearg
Ag lonrú
Ar dhuirling bháin,
Niamhrach an radharc,
San Earrach Thiar.
A man throwing / A basket from his back, / And the red seaweed / Shining / On a white rocky shore, / Wondrous the sight, / In the Western Spring.
Véarsa 3 · Verse 3
Mná i locháin
In íochtar diaidh-thrá,
A gcótaí craptha,
Scáileanna fúthu
Ag rince ar ghainimh,
San Earrach Thiar.
Women in pools / At the ebb of the tide, / Their skirts tucked up, / Shadows beneath them / Dancing on the sand, / In the Western Spring.
Véarsa 4 · Verse 4
Currach lán éisc
Ag teacht chun cladaigh
Ar órmhuir mhall
I ndeireadh lae,
San Earrach Thiar.
A currach full of fish / Coming to shore / On a slow, golden sea / At the end of day, / In the Western Spring.

💡 Leid don Scrúdú / Exam Tip

Is dán é seo faoi ghrá áite — grá an fhile d'Inis Mór. Bain úsáid as nathanna Gaeilge ó na véarsaí i do fhreagra agus tabhair an t-aistriúchán eatarthu sna lúibíní. Déan tagairt don athrá "San Earrach Thiar" ag deireadh gach véarsa agus mínigh cén fáth a bhfuil sé cumhachtach.

This poem is about love of place — the poet's love for Inis Mór. Use Irish phrases from the verses in your answer and give the translation in brackets. Always mention the repetition "San Earrach Thiar" and explain its effect.
