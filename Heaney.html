Quick Tip for the Exam 📝

Heaney's great subject is the ordinary made extraordinary. A forge, a pitchfork, an aunt baking — these are his raw material. Always show how he elevates the mundane through precise language, rich sound devices, and the weight of historical and personal memory. Examiners reward students who can connect the personal and the political in his work.
