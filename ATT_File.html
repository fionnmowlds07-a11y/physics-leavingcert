An File · The Poet

Máirtín Ó Direáin

Born on Inis Mór in 1910, Ó Direáin left the island at 18 and spent the rest of his life longing to return. His poetry is shaped entirely by that loss — and by his profound love for the Aran Islands.

1910–1988 Inis Mór · Oileáin Árann Filíocht / Poetry Gaeilge Árann

👤 Who Was Máirtín Ó Direáin?

Máirtín Ó Direáin is widely regarded as one of the greatest Irish-language poets of the twentieth century. He was born and raised on the island of Inis Mór in the Aran Islands — Rugadh é ar Inis Mór sna hOileáin Árann sa bhliain 1910 (He was born on Inis Mór in the Aran Islands in 1910) — and his entire literary output was shaped by his relationship with that island and the loss of the simple, communal life it represented.

His poetry is characterised by maoithneachas (sentimentality / deep feeling) — he was, as one student answer notes, "saghas maoithneach i gcuid dá dánta" (somewhat sentimental in some of his poems). This emotional intensity, rooted in personal loss, gives his work a lasting power.

📅 Timeline — Líne Ama

1910

Breitheanna / Born

Rugadh Máirtín Ó Direáin ar Inis Mór sna hOileáin Árann. He grew up speaking Irish as his first language, immersed in the traditional island culture of farming, fishing, and community.

1928

D'fhág sé an t-oileán / Left the Island

Nuair a bhí sé ocht mbliana déag d'aois, d'fhág sé an t-oileán chun dul ag obair (When he was eighteen, he left the island to go to work). He got a post in the GPO (Ard-Oifig an Phoist) in Galway city — a dramatic change from the wild Aran Islands.

1930s

Baile Átha Cliath / Dublin

He moved to Dublin to work in the civil service. Ní raibh sé tógtha le saol sa chathair (He was not taken with life in the city). The grey, bureaucratic urban world stood in sharp contrast to everything he had known and loved on Inis Mór.

1942

Céad Chnuasach / First Collection

Published Coinnle Geala (1942) and Dánta Aniar (1943) — his first poetry collections. The island and the longing for it are central themes.

1957

Ó Mórna agus Dánta Eile

Published one of his most celebrated collections. His island themes deepen and mature. He is now recognised as one of the leading voices in modern Irish-language poetry.

1961

Feamainn Bhealtaine

His only prose work — a novel. The title itself (Feamainn Bhealtaine — May Seaweed) reflects the island world he never stopped writing about.

1962

Ár Ré Dearóil agus Dánta Eile

Another major collection. The title translates as "Our Wretched Age" — the poet's disillusionment with the modern world is increasingly evident.

1988

Fuair sé bás / He Died

Fuair sé bás sa bhliain 1988 (He died in 1988). He spent most of his adult life away from Inis Mór but never forgot it — his last collections still return to the same island themes.

Model Biography Answer — What to Include

A full marks biography answer covers: (1) Born on Inis Mór in 1910. (2) Left the island at 18 to work in Galway/Dublin. (3) Was not happy in the city — missed island life. (4) His longing for the island shaped his poetry — he was quite sentimental. (5) Named at least two works with dates. (6) Died in 1988. Six facts, written fluently in Irish with translations.

📝 Model Biography Answer — Freagra Samhail

Scríobh Máirtín Ó Direáin an dán seo. (Máirtín Ó Direáin wrote this poem.) Rugadh é ar Inis Mór sna hOileáin Árann sa bhliain 1910. (He was born on Inis Mór in the Aran Islands in 1910.) Nuair a bhí sé ocht mbliana déag d'aois, d'fhág sé an t-oileán chun dul ag obair sa chathair. (When he was eighteen years old, he left the island to go working in the city.) Fuair sé post in Ard-Oifig an Phoist i gcathair na Gaillimhe mar fhear óg. (He got a post in the GPO in Galway city as a young man.)

Ní raibh sé tógtha le saol sa chathair. (He was not taken with life in the city.) D'fhéach sé ar an oileán mar áit cosúil le neamh agus bhí uaigneas air i ndiaidh an bhaile. (He looked at the island as a place like heaven and he was homesick.) Mar thoradh ar sin, bíonn sé saghas maoithneach i gcuid dá dánta. (As a result, he is quite sentimental in some of his poems.)

"Coinnle Geala" (1942), "Dánta Aniar" (1943), "Ó Mórna agus Dánta Eile" (1957) agus "Ár Ré Dearóil agus Dánta Eile" (1962) cuid dá chuid litríochta. (Some of his literature.) Scríobh sé úrscéal amháin freisin: "Feamainn Bhealtaine" (1961). (He also wrote one novel: "May Seaweed".) Fuair sé bás sa bhliain 1988. (He died in 1988.)
